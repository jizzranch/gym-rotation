# Gym Rotation

A home-screen workout tracker for iPhone. Tick off a workout and it moves to the bottom of the list, showing how many days since you last did it. The Analysis tab shows your gym visits this year, your weekly average, and charts.

**Open it:** https://jizzranch.github.io/gym-rotation/

**Install on iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then tap **Add to Home Screen**.

Your data stays on your own phone. Use Settings, then **Copy backup**, to keep a copy.
